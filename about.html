About this gallery

The Office of Sustainability works with University of St. Thomas capstone students to create short games that teach sustainability: recycling, energy, food, water and campus life. This gallery keeps every semester's games in one place.

For students: submitting your game

  1. Publish your game so it can be played in a browser (for example, with GitHub Pages).
  2. Send the Office of Sustainability:
    • Game title
    • Team member names
    • Semester (for example, Fall 2026)
    • Department
    • A one or two sentence description
    • A link to the playable game
    • A screenshot or image, 3:2 (optional)
