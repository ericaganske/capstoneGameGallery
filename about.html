About This Gallery

The University of St. Thomas Office of Sustainability partners with capstone students to develop interactive games that explore sustainability concepts and encourage thoughtful engagement with real-world challenges. Covering topics such as energy, waste, food systems, water conservation, and sustainable communities, these projects offer an engaging way to learn about sustainability through play.

This gallery brings together games developed across semesters, creating a central space to explore student projects, discover new perspectives, and see how sustainability can be approached through interactive experiences.

To make the collection easier to navigate, games are organized into eight sustainability categories: Clean Energy & Climate, Nature & Biodiversity, Water & Natural Resources, Waste & Circular Economy, Sustainable Cities & Communities, Food & Agriculture, People & Social Sustainability, and Sustainable Business & Innovation. These categories reflect the primary sustainability topics, challenges, and concepts explored within each game. Because sustainability issues often overlap, a game may appear in multiple categories when it meaningfully addresses more than one topic.

For Students: Submitting Your Game

To have your capstone game included in the gallery, please ensure it is published and accessible through a web browser, such as through GitHub Pages. Then submit the following information to the Office of Sustainability:

  • Game title
  • Team member names
  • Semester and year (e.g., Fall 2026)
  • Department
  • Game description: A brief, one- to two-sentence overview of the game's purpose, concept, or sustainability focus
  • Playable game link
  • Game screenshot or preview image: Optional, preferably in a 3:2 aspect ratio

Providing complete information helps ensure your project can be accurately represented and easily discovered within the gallery.
